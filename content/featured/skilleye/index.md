A skeleton-based swing quality analysis for tennis players. Determines swing quality by using a racket-mounted IMU board to capture wrist motion along with RTMPose to extract the player's 2D skeleton and using an ST-GCN to score the swing against expert templates. 

Finalist for the [2026 CTCI Science & Technology Creativity Competition](https://www.ctci.org.tw/8838/talent/41184/46745/46746/)
