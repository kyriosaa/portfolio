BitTheater is a compact, tactile device that brings custom animations to life, displaying up to 4 unique sequences in a sleek, pocket-sized design.

I developed BitTheater as an exercise in creating more permanent projects. Starting with a breadboard prototype and Arduino program, I progressed to soldering components onto a perfboard, and ultimately designed a custom PCB for the final build. This project allowed me to apply hardware skills from initial concept to a final polished product.
